><td>NULL이면 대체값</td><td><code>NVL(COMMISSION_PCT, 0)</code></td></tr>

<section id="join" data-subject="join">
<div class="section-title" onclick="toggleSection(this)"><h2>20. JOIN — 여러 테이블 연결</h2><span class="badge">Oracle / ANSI / OUTER / SELF</span></div>
<div class="section-body">
<div class="callout"><b>JOIN:</b> 서로 다른 테이블 2개 이상을 공통된 컬럼 값을 기준으로 가로로 이어 붙여 조회합니다.</div>
<table>
<tr><th>종류</th><th>언제 사용?</th><th>수업 핵심</th></tr>
<tr><td>INNER JOIN</td><td>양쪽에 연결되는 데이터만</td><td>기본 JOIN</td></tr>
<tr><td>LEFT OUTER JOIN</td><td>왼쪽 테이블은 전부</td><td>Oracle 문법에서는 없어도 되는 오른쪽에 <code>(+)</code></td></tr>
<tr><td>RIGHT OUTER JOIN</td><td>오른쪽 테이블은 전부</td><td>없어도 되는 왼쪽에 <code>(+)</code></td></tr>
<tr><td>FULL OUTER JOIN</td><td>양쪽 모두 전부</td><td>ANSI 문법 사용</td></tr>
<tr><td>SELF JOIN</td><td>같은 테이블을 자기 자신과 연결</td><td>직원 ↔ 매니저 등</td></tr>
</table>

<h3>Oracle JOIN 문법</h3>
<pre><button class="copy">복사</button><code>SELECT E.EMPLOYEE_ID,
       E.LAST_NAME,
       D.DEPARTMENT_NAME
  FROM EMPLOYEES E, DEPARTMENTS D
 WHERE E.DEPARTMENT_ID = D.DEPARTMENT_ID;</code></pre>

<h3>ANSI JOIN 문법</h3>
<pre><button class="copy">복사</button><code>SELECT E.LAST_NAME,
       D.DEPARTMENT_NAME
  FROM EMPLOYEES E
  JOIN DEPARTMENTS D
    ON E.DEPARTMENT_ID = D.DEPARTMENT_ID;</code></pre>

<h3>LEFT / RIGHT OUTER JOIN — Oracle (+)</h3>
<pre><button class="copy">복사</button><code>-- 직원은 모두 출력
SELECT E.EMPLOYEE_ID, E.LAST_NAME, D.DEPARTMENT_NAME
  FROM EMPLOYEES E, DEPARTMENTS D
 WHERE E.DEPARTMENT_ID = D.DEPARTMENT_ID(+);

-- 부서는 모두 출력
SELECT E.EMPLOYEE_ID, E.LAST_NAME, D.DEPARTMENT_NAME
  FROM EMPLOYEES E, DEPARTMENTS D
 WHERE E.DEPARTMENT_ID(+) = D.DEPARTMENT_ID;</code></pre>

<h3>FULL OUTER JOIN — ANSI</h3>
<pre><button class="copy">복사</button><code>SELECT E.LAST_NAME,
       D.DEPARTMENT_NAME
  FROM EMPLOYEES E
  FULL OUTER JOIN DEPARTMENTS D
    ON E.DEPARTMENT_ID = D.DEPARTMENT_ID;</code></pre>

<h3>SELF JOIN</h3>
<pre><button class="copy">복사</button><code>SELECT E.LAST_NAME AS 직원,
       M.LAST_NAME AS 매니저
  FROM EMPLOYEES E, EMPLOYEES M
 WHERE E.MANAGER_ID = M.EMPLOYEE_ID;</code></pre>
<div class="danger"><b>JOIN 실수:</b> 두 테이블에 같은 이름의 컬럼이 있으면 <code>E.DEPARTMENT_ID</code>처럼 테이블 별칭을 붙이는 습관이 중요합니다.</div>
</div></section>

<section id="groupjoin" data-subject="join">
<div class="section-title" onclick="toggleSection(this)"><h2>21. JOIN + GROUP BY + HAVING</h2><span class="badge">AVG / GROUP BY / HAVING</span></div>
<div class="section-body">
<div class="callout"><b>문제:</b> 평균 급여가 8000 이상인 부서의 부서 이름과 평균 급여를 조회.</div>
<pre><button class="copy">복사</button><code>SELECT D.DEPARTMENT_NAME,
       ROUND(AVG(E.SALARY), 1) AS 평균급여
  FROM EMPLOYEES E, DEPARTMENTS D
 WHERE E.DEPARTMENT_ID = D.DEPARTMENT_ID
 GROUP BY D.DEPARTMENT_NAME
HAVING AVG(E.SALARY) &gt;= 8000;</code></pre>
<table>
<tr><th>절</th><th>역할</th></tr>
<tr><td>WHERE</td><td>JOIN 연결 조건 / 행 조건</td></tr>
<tr><td>GROUP BY</td><td>부서 이름 기준 그룹화</td></tr>
<tr><td>AVG()</td><td>그룹별 평균 계산</td></tr>
<tr><td>HAVING</td><td>그룹 함수 결과에 조건</td></tr>
</table>
</div></section>
